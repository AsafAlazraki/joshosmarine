0212V installs

12 volts. Zero guesswork.

Most electrical gremlins on boats come down to the same three things: undersized cable, corroded joins and circuits nobody labelled. Every install is built to avoid all of them.

  • A

    Tinned marine cable

    Sized for the load and the length of the run, so full voltage actually reaches the gear.

  • B

    Every circuit fused

    Individually protected and labelled at the panel, so a fault is found in minutes rather than a weekend.

  • C

    Sealed connections

    Crimped, heat-shrunk and kept up out of the bilge, where salt can't get at them.

  • D

    Looms you can follow

    Neat, supported cable runs that you, or the next person, can trace from end to end.

Popular installs

  • Dual-battery systems
  • Lithium conversions
  • Sounder + GPS combos
  • Bow-mount electric motors
  • Switch panel upgrades
  • Marine stereos
  • Deck & underwater lights
  • USB & 12V sockets
  • Full rewires

06Good to know

Fair questions.

Can't see yours here? Ask it in the booking form or give us a call.

Do I need to know exactly what's wrong before I book?

Not at all. Describe what it's doing, or not doing, and add the make and model if you know them. We'll work out the rest. Photos and short videos help a lot.

How much will it cost?

It depends on the job, but you'll always get a quote before any work starts. If something unexpected turns up along the way, we'll call you before going any further.

Can you install gear I've already bought?

Usually, yes. Let us know what it is when you book so we can check it suits your boat and turn up with the right cable, fuses and fittings.

Which makes and models do you work on?

Most of the common boat, outboard and trailer brands. Put the details in the booking form and we'll confirm before you make the trip.

How long will the job take?

Smaller installs and most trailer jobs are quick. Full rewires and custom fit-outs take longer. You'll get a realistic timeframe with your quote, and we'll keep you posted if anything changes.

Do you come to me?

Depending on the job and where you are, often we can. Tick "come to me" in the booking form and we'll let you know what's possible.
