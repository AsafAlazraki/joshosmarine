02Inside the hub

Where the salt gets in.

Wheel bearings are the most common reason a boat trailer ends up on the side of the road. This is what is inside each hub, and the one part that keeps the water out.

Swipe sideways to see the whole hub →

  • /01

    Hot hub, cold water

    A hub is warm after a tow. Back it into the water and it cools quickly, which draws water in past the seal.

  • /02

    Water turns grease to paste

    Once salt water is inside, the grease breaks down and the bearing starts to pit and rust.

  • /03

    A seal is cheap. A hub is not

    New seals at each service keep the water out. Left alone, a failed bearing can take the hub and the axle with it.

Early warnings

  • 01A hub that is hot to touch after a tow
  • 02Grease flung on the inside of the wheel or guard
  • 03Rumbling or grinding from a wheel
  • 04Brakes that drag, grab or do nothing
  • 05Lights that fail after every launch
  • 06The boat sits crooked, or is hard to winch on
