02Service rhythm

How often it's due.

An outboard that lives in salt water needs looking after on a schedule, whether or not it has done many hours.

  1. You

    Every trip

    Flush the motor with fresh water, check the telltale is running strong, and look for oil or fishing line around the prop.

  2. The service

    100 hours or once a year, whichever comes first

    Oils, filters, plugs, gearbox oil and anodes, plus a check of everything that moves.

  3. The bigger items

    Every few seasons

    Water pump impeller, thermostat and fuel lines, sooner if the boat works hard or sits for long stretches.

Intervals vary from motor to motor. Your owner's manual has the schedule for yours, and we work to that.
